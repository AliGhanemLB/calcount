🥗 Mild Cut—
300 kcal below maintenance — the science-backed sweet spot for fat loss without muscle sacrifice.
Pros
  • Preserves muscle mass
  • Sustainable long-term
  • ~0.3 kg/week loss
Cons
  • Slower visible results
  • Requires patience
⚡ Aggressive Cut—
500 kcal below maintenance — clinical sweet spot for 0.5 kg/week loss.
Pros
  • ~0.5 kg/week loss
  • Visible in weeks
  • Good for short phases
Cons
  • Harder to sustain
  • Some muscle risk
  • May feel fatigued
📈 Lean Bulk—
250 kcal above maintenance — clean gains with minimal fat. The long game.
Pros
  • Mostly muscle gain
  • Stay relatively lean
  • Sustainable year-round
Cons
  • Slower mass gain
  • Requires clean diet
🍔 Dirty Bulk—
500 kcal above maintenance — max muscle, eat everything, sort the fat out later.
Pros
  • Fast strength gains
  • High energy
  • Eat what you want
Cons
  • Significant fat gain
  • Need a cut after
  • Not ideal long-term
